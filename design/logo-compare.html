A — straight segments

It is what the app actually draws: connector() traces a straight line, not a curve. The 45° bends give the anchors an obvious place to exist.

Risk: with a horizontal middle, at 16px it comes close to a “5”. How tight the middle gap is set is what decides it.

B — continuous curve

Two tangent ellipses (rx 14, ry 10) — the seam in the centre is horizontal on both, so the curve runs smooth. It reads as a letter before it reads as a diagram.

Risk: the arrow on the tail ends up pointing up-left and can read as an “undo” icon. Shortening the tail fixes it.
